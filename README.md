# Genesis Mirrors

An interactive, single-page website about two chiasms (mirror structures) in the book of Genesis, in English and Spanish.

## What's on the page

**The Flood (Genesis 6:9–9:17)**
- The 77 verses split as 38 + 1 + 38, with "God remembered Noah" (8:1) as verse 39, exactly in the middle.
- An arc diagram: one square per verse, with rainbow arcs joining the eight pairs of scenes that mirror each other. Tap a pair to highlight it.
- The day counts that come back in reverse order: 7 · 7 · 40 · 150 | 8:1 | 150 · 40 · 7 · 7.

**The Tower of Babel (Genesis 11:1–9)**
- The chiasm drawn as a ziggurat: the builders' half on the left, God's answer on the right, "The LORD came down" at the top. A play button lights the story up and back down.
- The Hebrew text with a translation, and a grid of the key words that repeat (*kol ha'arets*, *safah*, *sham*, …). Pick a word to highlight it in both languages.
- The wordplay (*nilbenah* L‑B‑N becomes *navlah* N‑B‑L), the phrases that return later in the story, and the parallel pattern that runs alongside the mirror.

## Viewing it

Open `index.html` in a browser. It is one self-contained file with no build step; the only external resources are fonts from Google Fonts.

Use the **English / Español** buttons in the top bar to switch language. The choice is remembered in the browser. The page follows the system's light or dark mode and works at phone width.

## Sources

- Flood structure after Gordon Wenham, "The Coherence of the Flood Narrative", *Vetus Testamentum* 28 (1978). The scene boundaries are simplified and fitted to 6:9–9:17.
- Babel structure after J. P. Fokkelman, *Narrative Art in Genesis* (1975), and Gordon Wenham, *Genesis 1–15* (Word Biblical Commentary, 1987), simplified.
- Hebrew: the Masoretic Text. The English and Spanish translations are fairly literal translations made for this page.

The verse divisions come from the Masoretes and the verse numbers were added in the Middle Ages, so the exact 38 + 1 + 38 count may reflect how the text was divided rather than the original author's design. The page says so too.
